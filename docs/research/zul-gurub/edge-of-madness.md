## Overview
- Four alternative bosses, one per lockout, summoned at the Edge of Madness (turn right at the crossroads past Zanza's Rise) by using Gurubashi Mojo Madness (Alchemy 300 recipe learned from the tablet behind the brazier; Blood of Heroes + Massive Mojo + 6 Powerful Mojo + Black Lotus) on the brazier. Which boss appears is fixed by the calendar on the day the raid ID was created: Gri'lek → Hazza'rah → Renataki → Wushoolay, 14 days each (56-day cycle, flips at 00:00 Pacific). OPTIONAL: no effect on Hakkar. Each drops its own trinket piece (Gri'lek's Blood, Hazza'rah's Dream Thread, Renataki's Tooth, Wushoolay's Mane) for the class trinkets.
- Trash: Mad Servants (imps, level 61 elite, 12,575 HP, Fireball 510 + DoT, Flamestrike 1,157 + 75/2 s) that open a Portal of Madness releasing elite Voidwalkers on death — kill one at a time, Banish/Enslave imps, voidwalkers cannot be banished.
- Levels/HP: all level 63 World Boss; Gri'lek 333,100; Hazza'rah 266,500 (5,240 mana); Renataki 333,100; Wushoolay 293,150 (31,440 mana). No enrage timers.
- Raid-size assumptions: 20-man Classic Era. Season of Discovery lets the raid choose the boss (wiki).

## Gri'lek the Wanderer

### Abilities

#### Avatar
- Name: Avatar (24646)
- Type: enrage + threat wipe + pursuit
- Target rule: self; then pursues a random player (Wowhead; wiki: pre-1.10.2 fixate, since 1.10.2 "does not fixate on one target" — ping-pong him)
- Cast time: instant
- Cooldown or cadence: "about half the time" he is in Avatar (wiki); interval unknown
- Damage: +500% physical damage (hits ~5,000+), size +200%, movement -75%; 15 s
- Debuff: n/a
- Movement effect: threat wiped (Wowhead, wiki); he lumbers slowly after his target — kite, everyone stays out of melee until it ends
- Telegraph: he grows huge and announces/targets a player (wiki "announces his targeted player")

#### Entangling Roots
- Name: Entangling Roots (24648)
- Type: targeted root
- Target rule: his pursued/current target, 30 yd
- Cast time: instant
- Cooldown or cadence: unknown
- Damage: 219 Nature every 3 s
- Debuff: Root 10 s, Magic — priests/paladins dispel the rooted target immediately (otherwise Avatar catches and one-shots them)
- Movement effect: root
- Telegraph: none

#### Ground Tremor
- Name: Ground Tremor (6524)
- Type: AoE stun
- Target rule: all within 20 yd
- Cast time: instant
- Cooldown or cadence: 5 s cooldown
- Damage: none
- Debuff: stun 2 s
- Movement effect: stun
- Telegraph: none; stay >20 yd unless melee/tank

#### Sweeping Strikes / Stun
- Name: Sweeping Strikes (18765), Stun (24647)
- Type: melee
- Target rule: next 5 melee hits also hit an extra nearby enemy (20 s); 2 s self-stun aura 24647 (likely cosmetic/used on transitions)
- Cast time: instant
- Cooldown or cadence: unknown
- Damage: extra cleave hits
- Debuff: none
- Movement effect: none
- Telegraph: none

### Adds
- None.

### Positioning and movement
- Tank: he is taunt-immune (wiki). Tank normally between Avatars; when Avatar starts, back off — threat is gone and he hits 5x. Pick him up again when he shrinks.
- Melee DPS: on him outside Avatar; run out at Avatar (he is slowed 75%, so anyone un-rooted escapes).
- Ranged DPS: spread >20 yd (Ground Tremor); DPS through Avatar while kiting; whoever he pursues runs away from the raid.
- Healers: dispel Entangling Roots the instant it lands on the pursued player; HoTs on the runner.
- Whole raid: open area, no LoS tricks; "ping-pong" him between two groups during Avatar.

### What each role must do and must not do
- Tank: (1) establish normally; (2) at Avatar, stop and move away — don't try to hold him; (3) re-engage after 15 s. Never stand in Avatar melee range.
- DPS: (1) never be within 20 yd clumped (tremor); (2) when pursued, run — don't fight; (3) keep DPS up during Avatar from range.
- Healer: (1) dispel roots immediately (Magic); (2) heal the runner; (3) keep tanks topped after Avatar re-engages.

### Timeline of a typical pull
- t=0 pull; normal melee boss with Sweeping Strikes and tremor stuns on melee.
- Periodic Avatar (15 s): threat wipe, +500% damage, slow pursuit of a random player + roots; raid kites and dispels; after 15 s re-tank. Repeat until 333k HP is gone.

### Open questions
- Avatar cadence not published; "half the time" (wiki).
- Fixate vs random re-targeting (pre/post 1.10.2).
- Whether Stun 24647 is used on players.

## Hazza'rah the Dreamweaver

### Abilities

#### Chain Burn (Mana Burn)
- Name: Chain Burn (24684)
- Type: chain mana burn
- Target rule: a player, chains through nearby raid members (wiki/Wowhead)
- Cast time: instant, 100 yd
- Cooldown or cadence: unknown; requires him to have mana (pool only 5,240)
- Damage: burns 1,313 mana per hit (DBC) — damage from the burn not listed
- Debuff: none
- Movement effect: none
- Telegraph: none; prevented entirely by keeping him at 0 mana (Viper Sting, Drain Mana, Mana Burn)

#### Sleep (Mass Sleep)
- Name: Sleep (24664)
- Type: AoE (room)
- Target rule: everyone within 100 yd
- Cast time: instant
- Cooldown or cadence: periodic, unknown
- Damage: none
- Debuff: asleep 6 s; any damage wakes the target (so tanks wake instantly); Tremor Totem/Divine Shield break it
- Movement effect: incapacitate
- Telegraph: none; keep HoTs rolling on the tank

#### Earth Shock
- Name: Earth Shock (24685)
- Type: targeted
- Target rule: current target, 20 yd
- Cast time: instant
- Cooldown or cadence: unknown
- Damage: 1,094 Nature
- Debuff: interrupts casting for 4 s
- Movement effect: none
- Telegraph: none

#### Summon Nightmare Illusions
- Name: Summon Nightmare Illusions (24728 + 24681 + 24729)
- Type: summon adds
- Target rule: 3 illusions, each fixated on a raid member (wiki)
- Cast time: instant
- Cooldown or cadence: periodic, unknown ("periodically summons three")
- Damage: n/a
- Debuff: n/a
- Movement effect: n/a
- Telegraph: illusions appear (felguard/abomination/lasher/devilsaur models)

### Adds
- Nightmare Illusions (NPC 15163) x3 per summon: level 60, 458 HP (wiki 500), AoE-immune, hit for ~5,000 physical before armor (wiki) — one or two shots kill cloth. Die to any single hit (Multi-Shot, Fire Blast, Exorcism works). Assign 3–4 ranged (hunters/mages) as illusion killers; Stoneclaw Totem buys time.

### Positioning and movement
- Tank: tank him normally; expect to be slept but woken by his own hits; keep HoTs.
- Melee DPS: on him; casters out of melee (the Tentonhammer/Warcraft Tavern note "sleep AoE" — DBC says 100 yd anyway).
- Ranged DPS: spread so Chain Burn jumps less; hunters Viper Sting + warlocks Drain Mana from the first second; illusion squad on call.
- Healers: HoTs on the tank before each sleep; keep mana (Chain Burn).
- Whole raid: drain him to 0 mana immediately and keep him there; kill illusions in one global.

### What each role must do and must not do
- Tank: (1) pick up; (2) healthstone/potion ready for sleeps; (3) never fight without HoTs.
- DPS: (1) mana-drain him first; (2) illusion killers react instantly; (3) high DPS so healers don't go dry.
- Healer: (1) pre-HoT before sleeps; (2) stay spread (Chain Burn); (3) Tremor Totem for sleep.

### Timeline of a typical pull
- t=0 pull; Viper Sting/Drain Mana on; he burns mana until empty (~4 Chain Burns at 1,313).
- Periodic Mass Sleep 6 s (tank wakes); periodic 3 illusions — kill in one global each; Earth Shocks on the tank. ~266k HP.

### Open questions
- Sleep radius/duration vs guides: DBC 100 yd/6 s; wiki "all opponents in range".
- Illusion HP 458 (DB) vs 500 (wiki); their damage "~5,000" (wiki) not verifiable.
- Cadences of Sleep/illusions unknown.

## Renataki of the Thousand Blades

### Abilities

#### Gouge
- Name: Gouge (24698)
- Type: melee AoE incapacitate
- Target rule: all within 8 yd (precedes Vanish)
- Cast time: instant
- Cooldown or cadence: before each Vanish (wiki/Wowhead)
- Damage: 20
- Debuff: incapacitate 4 s (breaks on damage)
- Movement effect: melee frozen
- Telegraph: he gouges the melee pack → mages reposition to re-find him

#### Vanish → Ambush
- Name: Vanish (24699) → Vanish teleport (24700) → Ambush (24337 per Wowhead; Thrash on arrival per cmangos)
- Type: teleport / stealth / burst
- Target rule: random player
- Cast time: instant; stealth up to 20 s
- Cooldown or cadence: "regular intervals" (Millenium); unknown
- Damage: Ambush "one-shots cloth" (Wowhead), two-shots cloth (wiki); teleport applies a 2 s stun + threat on the victim
- Debuff: stun 2 s
- Movement effect: he appears behind the victim; threat changes to that player (24700 threat effect)
- Telegraph: he disappears; hunter/warlock pets keep following him — follow the pets and AoE their spot; any AoE (even Demoralizing Shout) breaks stealth; DoTs do not

#### Thousand Blades
- Name: Thousand Blades (24649)
- Type: AoE (room)
- Target rule: every player (script effect fires it at all players; 100 yd)
- Cast time: 0.25 s
- Cooldown or cadence: unknown
- Damage: 1,094 Physical per player
- Debuff: none
- Movement effect: none
- Telegraph: brief cast; spread out doesn't help (hits all) — heal through

#### Enrage / Thrash
- Name: Enrage (8269), Thrash (3391)
- Type: enrage / melee
- Target rule: self (wiki lists Enrage; threshold unknown — Cataclysm version 30%)
- Cast time: instant
- Cooldown or cadence: once
- Damage: +60% attack speed, +50 physical damage for 2 min; Thrash 2 extra attacks
- Debuff: Enrage type (Tranquilizing Shot may remove — unverified)
- Movement effect: none
- Telegraph: enrage visual

### Adds
- None.

### Positioning and movement
- Tank: rogues can tank him (wiki) or a warrior; after each Vanish the OT with the casters picks him up when he ambushes and brings him back.
- Melee DPS: on him; expect Gouge then Vanish — when gouged, do nothing; AoE classes spread to the edges to break stealth.
- Ranged DPS: casters grouped with an off-tank; mages at the group edges ready to Arcane Explosion/Blizzard where the pets run; hunters Flare/mark.
- Healers: grouped with casters; heal the Ambush victim (if alive) and Thousand Blades (~1,100 raid-wide).
- Whole raid: one tight group + melee pack; pets on him at all times.

### What each role must do and must not do
- Tank: (1) hold him; (2) OT with casters taunts on ambush; (3) Demoralizing Shout to unstealth.
- DPS: (1) pets on him always; (2) AoE his location the moment he vanishes; (3) don't stand alone.
- Healer: (1) stay in the group; (2) raid heals for Thousand Blades; (3) prepare for the Ambush victim.

### Timeline of a typical pull
- t=0 pull; melee on him; Thousand Blades periodically (1,094 to all).
- Gouge the melee → Vanish (≤20 s) → AoE/pets reveal him or he Ambushes a random player → OT grabs, back to position. Repeat. Enrage late (threshold unknown). ~333k HP.

### Open questions
- Ambush damage/spell: Wowhead links 24337 (not fetched); cmangos substitutes Thrash + 2 s stun.
- Enrage threshold (Classic) unknown.
- Vanish cadence unknown.

## Wushoolay the Storm-Witch

### Abilities

#### Chain Lightning
- Name: Chain Lightning (24680)
- Type: chain (frontal per Wowhead)
- Target rule: a target in front within 30 yd, jumps to nearby enemies, up to 10 targets with increasing damage (wiki)
- Cast time: 1.5 s (interruptible)
- Cooldown or cadence: unknown; costs 150 mana
- Damage: 875 Nature base, rising per jump
- Debuff: none
- Movement effect: none
- Telegraph: cast bar; melee stay behind him, casters spread

#### Forked Lightning
- Name: Forked Lightning (24682)
- Type: frontal cone
- Target rule: enemies in a cone in front, 30 yd
- Cast time: instant; 150 mana
- Cooldown or cadence: unknown
- Damage: 1,157 Nature (1,157–1,343)
- Debuff: none
- Movement effect: none
- Telegraph: none; face him away from the raid

#### Lightning Cloud
- Name: Lightning Cloud (24683 / 25033)
- Type: ground hazard
- Target rule: a point under a player within 30 yd (often melee/tank)
- Cast time: instant; 60 mana
- Cooldown or cadence: unknown
- Damage: 463 Nature on cast (463–537) then 875 Nature (875–1,125) every 5 s for 15 s to anyone in the 10 yd radius (25033 variant: 657 + 657/5 s)
- Debuff: none (standing damage)
- Movement effect: move out immediately; tank drags him out of it
- Telegraph: visible cloud

#### Lightning Wave
- Name: Lightning Wave (24819)
- Type: targeted
- Target rule: random, 100 yd
- Cast time: instant
- Cooldown or cadence: unknown
- Damage: 463 Nature
- Debuff: none
- Movement effect: none
- Telegraph: none

### Adds
- None.

### Positioning and movement
- Tank: face him away from the raid (Forked/Chain Lightning frontal); when a cloud lands under you, walk him out together with melee. Nature Resistance gear/totem/Aspect of the Wild for the tank.
- Melee DPS: strictly behind him; move as a pack out of clouds; join only after his mana is drained (Millenium).
- Ranged DPS: spread (chain jumps); hunters Viper Sting + warlocks/priests drain his 31,440 mana so Chain/Forked Lightning stop.
- Healers: focus the tank (lightning bursts can kill a tank quickly); NR totem.
- Whole raid: spread, behind him, out of clouds.

### What each role must do and must not do
- Tank: (1) face away; (2) move out of clouds; (3) NR gear.
- DPS: (1) drain mana first; (2) never in front; (3) out of clouds instantly.
- Healer: (1) tank focus; (2) spread; (3) NR/Poison Cleansing totems (he carries a poison cloud per wiki).

### Timeline of a typical pull
- t=0 pull; drain mana; Forked/Chain Lightning on the front arc, Lightning Clouds under melee every so often (15 s each), Lightning Waves on random players. Once drained, melee burn him. ~293k HP.

### Open questions
- Chain Lightning frontal (Wowhead) vs normal chain (wiki/DBC).
- Two Lightning Cloud spell variants (24683/25033) — which is used.
- No cadences.
