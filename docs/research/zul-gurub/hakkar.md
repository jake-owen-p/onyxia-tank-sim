## Overview
- Level 63 ("??"), World Boss. Health 719,550 (warcraftdb Era, with no priests alive); wiki ~745,804 and roughly +50,000 per living High Priest (cmangos: a "Hakkar Power" stack per living priest). Mana 262,000 (unused). Enrage at 10 minutes in combat or below 5% health (+50% damage, +65% attack speed, 90 s) — treated as a wipe unless he is about to die. Final boss; the five High Priests must be dead or he keeps their Aspects.
- Phases: single phase on a 90 s Blood Siphon cycle. Blood Siphon is the fight: the raid must be carrying Poisonous Blood (from a dead Son of Hakkar's cloud) when it lands so the siphon damages him instead of healing him.
- Raid-size assumptions: 20-man Classic Era. SoD: killing him with all five priests alive drops "Bloodied" weapons (wiki); other SoD tuning not researched.

## Abilities

### Blood Siphon
- Name: Blood Siphon (24324 cast → 24322 on unpoisoned players / 24323 on poisoned players)
- Type: AoE (room) channel + stun
- Target rule: wiki: "players in a cone in front of him"; Warcraft Tavern: "everyone in front of Hakkar"; Wowhead/Amazon Basin: "the entire raid"; DBC: 100 yd radius script. Son of Hakkar wiki: does not hit every raid member.
- Cast time: instant start, 8 s channel
- Cooldown or cadence: every 90 s, first at 90 s after the pull (Tentonhammer, wiki, Amazon Basin, Warcraft Tavern)
- Damage: each affected player is stunned 8 s and loses 200 HP per second (1,600 over the channel); unpoisoned → Hakkar gains 1,000 HP per second per player (tooltip "Feeding Hakkar 1000 health per second"; Amazon Basin/Millenium) — with 20 unpoisoned players ~160,000 (Tentonhammer "~15%"); poisoned → the player still loses 200 Nature/s but Hakkar takes 200 damage per second per player instead (wiki, Amazon Basin) — Tentonhammer "up to ~10% of his health" per siphon. Wiki: no more than 3 unpoisoned players or he nets a heal.
- Debuff: Blood Siphon 8 s; the siphon "removes most existing debuffs" (wiki) and players in the cloud are cured when it ends (Tentonhammer) — Poisonous Blood must be re-applied every cycle.
- Movement effect: stun 8 s — no healing, no actions
- Telegraph: boss mod timer (CTRA/DBM) at 90 s intervals; the drain beams change colour when poisoned players are being drained (Tentonhammer)

### Corrupted Blood
- Name: Corrupted Blood (24328)
- Type: targeted AoE DoT
- Target rule: a random player within 100 yd and everyone within 10 yd of them (Tentonhammer: 15 yd)
- Cast time: instant
- Cooldown or cadence: roughly every 20 s (Wowhead); wiki: every 20–30 s
- Damage: 875 Shadow (875–1,125) + 200 Shadow every 2 s for 10 s (~2,000 total per player)
- Debuff: Corrupted Blood 10 s; dispel type none in DBC (wiki: "cleanse speed" — historic); does not spread since patch 1.9 (wiki); Wowhead text still says it spreads to nearby players (the 10 yd splash)
- Movement effect: none
- Telegraph: none. Spread 10+ yd so only one or two people take it; healers budget 15,000–30,000 healing per cast if the raid is stacked (wiki).

### Cause Insanity (tank mind control)
- Name: Cause Insanity (24327)
- Type: mind control
- Target rule: the player at the top of his threat list (the current tank), 30 yd
- Cast time: instant
- Cooldown or cadence: wiki every 10–15 s; Amazon Basin ~15 s; Tentonhammer: cast ~15 s after the previous expires (≈25 s between casts)
- Damage: none; the MC'd player has +100% melee attack speed and +150% movement speed (DBC; wiki alternative +50%/+80%) for 10 s
- Debuff: charm 10 s, NOT dispellable — Polymorph the MC'd tank, dispel the sheep when MC ends
- Movement effect: Hakkar moves to the next player on threat; MC does not reset the MT's threat (Wowhead; wiki says they "temporarily lose aggro and regain it")
- Telegraph: none; 2–3 tanks keep threat #1–#3 so he always goes to another tank; Hakkar is immune to taunt

## Adds
- Sons of Hakkar (NPC 11357): level 60 elite wind serpents, 15,260 HP (wiki ~14,685), standing on the two platforms on the stairs left and right of his room; they respawn quickly (wiki). Knockdown (60 damage DBC / 289 wiki, 2 s stun, up to 3 targets, 5 s cd), Thrash. On death: Summon Poison Cloud → a Poisonous Cloud (NPC 14989, 8 yd radius script, lasts ~10 s per the summon duration) that applies Poisonous Blood: 100 Nature per 3 s for 90 s (Wowhead 1.5 min; Tentonhammer "1 minute"), dispel type Poison — do NOT cleanse it. A hunter pulls one Son into the raid (alternating platforms); either kill it on arrival (if ≤90 s before the next siphon) or Polymorph/Hibernate it next to the raid and kill it 10–15 s before the siphon; everyone except the current tank steps into the cloud. Pull the next Son right after each siphon; never pull a fresh Son in the last seconds before a siphon (it could break CC while the raid is stunned).

## Positioning and movement
- Tank: 2–3 tanks all building threat from the pull (taunt-immune). MT faces him away from the raid at the top of the Altar of Blood; the other tanks sit at threat #2/#3 near him. When the MT is mind-controlled (every ~15–25 s) the next tank is already being hit; a mage sheeps the MC'd tank, priest dispels the sheep at 10 s, MT resumes. The tank currently holding him stays OUT of the poison cloud (wiki, Millenium) — he is the only unpoisoned player allowed (he still gets siphoned but the heal from one player is small). Reset: pull him down the stairs.
- Melee DPS: behind him, spread so a Corrupted Blood on one of you doesn't splash the others (10 yd); rogues on the "center boards" help kill Sons (Tentonhammer). After each siphon everyone bandages.
- Ranged DPS: spread to both sides, closer to Hakkar than usual so Blood Siphon's cone definitely covers you (wiki) — if the siphon is a frontal cone, standing behind him means you are not drained but also do not damage him; 10+ yd between players for Corrupted Blood. Warriors keep Intimidating Shout on cooldown (so an MC'd warrior cannot fear the raid); mages: one on Son-sheep duty, two on tank-sheep duty.
- Healers: at least 6; three on the tanks (rotating with threat order), the rest spot-heal and the puller/sheepers; priests in the centre rear so they reach the cloud quickly (wiki). Top the raid in the ~5 s before each siphon (siphon + poison = 300/s per player for 8 s). Lightwell/bandages after each drain. Do not cleanse Poisonous Blood; do not decurse/cleanse anything during the stun (you can't).
- Whole raid: fight on the platform in front of his altar; the Son cloud is dropped at a marked spot in the middle of the raid; everybody except the active tank stands in it for a few seconds, then returns to spread positions before the siphon.

## What each role must do and must not do
- Tank:
  1. Three tanks build threat from second 1; never let a non-tank reach #2.
  2. Pre-cast Intimidating Shout and keep it on cooldown through the fight.
  3. When MC'd: accept the sheep; when it breaks, resume; the next tank holds without taunting (impossible).
  4. The active tank stays out of the poison cloud and out of its 8 yd radius.
  5. Never stand in the raid (Cleave) and never die — a resurrected tank cannot regain enough threat (wiki).
- DPS:
  1. Poisonous Blood on you before every siphon is the single most important job (Wowhead).
  2. Don't damage the sheeped Son until 10–15 s before the siphon (cloud lasts ~10 s; debuff 90 s).
  3. Spread 10 yd for Corrupted Blood; stop DPS during tank MCs until the next tank is settled (Tentonhammer).
  4. Ramp DPS with the siphons so he dies before 10 minutes; a poisoned siphon takes ~10% off him.
  5. Hunters: pull Sons alternating left/right platform; Feign Death as needed. Mages: sheep the Son and the MC'd tank.
- Healer:
  1. Pre-heal the raid before each 90 s siphon; everyone loses 1,600 during it and cannot be healed.
  2. Heal Corrupted Blood targets (~2,000 each) and tanks under Enrage/Aspect effects.
  3. Dispel the sheep off the tank when MC ends; never cleanse Poisonous Blood.
  4. Stand in the cloud like everyone else (you are drained too).

## Timeline of a typical pull
- t=0: MT pulls on the altar; 2 OTs build threat; hunter pulls Son #1 from a platform → mage sheeps it next to the raid.
- t≈15–25 s: first Cause Insanity on the MT (10 s): sheep, OT holds; Corrupted Blood on a random player every ~20 s from here on.
- t≈75–80 s: kill Son #1; raid (minus active tank) stands in the cloud → Poisonous Blood (100/3 s, 90 s).
- t=90 s: Blood Siphon #1 — 8 s stun; poisoned players lose 200/s and he takes 200/s per poisoned player (~30k+ with 19 poisoned); unpoisoned players heal him 1,000/s each. Bandage after.
- t≈95 s: hunter pulls Son #2, sheep it; DPS resumes (DoTs/light DPS until tanks are well ahead on threat).
- t≈165 s: kill Son #2, cloud. t=180 s: Siphon #2. Repeat at 270, 360, 450, 540 s.
- t=600 s: Enrage (+50% damage, +65% haste) — he must be dead or at 1–2% by now. Typical kills come after 4–6 siphons (~6–9 minutes) with 719k–745k HP.
- Death: Heart of Hakkar drops; +400 Zandalar reputation.
