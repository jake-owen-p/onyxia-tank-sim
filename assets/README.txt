Static art only.

favicon.svg  - the ember dragon-eye mark used as the browser tab icon.

There are no audio files in this project: every warning cue, countdown tick
and voice line is synthesised at runtime with WebAudio (src/audio/cues.js),
and the voice countdown uses the browser's speechSynthesis when available.
Ability and boss icons are drawn with Canvas paths in src/render/shapes.js.
